- Add support for Grafana's `Recovering` instance state ("keep firing for"): a first-seen recovering instance is classified preexisting, and `check` observes it through its own recovery deadline so a genuine recovery reads `recovered` while an unresolved, paused, or absent one stays `still_failing`.
- Remove `watch --poll-interval`. Every rule now polls at exactly half its evaluation interval, so a state that lasts a full interval can never fall between polls; the flag could only widen `maxGap` and hide transitions.
